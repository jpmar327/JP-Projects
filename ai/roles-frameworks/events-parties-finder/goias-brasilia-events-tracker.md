## Latest Results — Trindade, GO

**Search window covered:** July 25, 2026 – September 25, 2026
**Last updated:** July 25, 2026

**No matching events found.** Trindade is a small municipality (~155,000 people) best known for the Festa do Divino Pai Eterno, one of Brazil's largest pilgrimage festivals — but that runs late June to early July, before this window, and is religious/sertanejo in character anyway (excluded genres). Checked Shotgun, Sympla, VibeIndex (which doesn't even list Trindade among its Goiás cities with active events), and local Trindade community sites — none show any house/techno/hip-hop/funk/rap listings in the July 25–Sept 25, 2026 window. Worth re-checking closer to the date, since small-city nightlife tends to be promoted late and locally (Instagram/WhatsApp) rather than on ticketing platforms.
